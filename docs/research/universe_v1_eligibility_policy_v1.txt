RESEARCH INTERPRETATION

Primary comparable research population:
8 symbols

12 symbols

The 8-symbol primary population is the cleanest common population
for cross-asset scorer and strategy discrimination.

The four secondary symbols may be used for robustness checks and
expanded evidence, but should remain distinguishable from the
primary population.

Insufficient or boundary-incompatible symbols remain valid members
of Universe V1. They are excluded only from experiments requiring
the current common walk-forward contract.


IMPORTANT FINDING

Raw historical coverage alone is not sufficient for eligibility.

Some symbols retain high stored-bar coverage but have thousands of
physical gaps. Repeated gap boundaries require independent warmup
and substantially reduce structurally eligible observations.

Eligibility therefore uses both:

stored coverage
and
structurally eligible coverage


HISTORICAL AUDIT FIX VERIFIED

During eligibility analysis, BICO exposed a valid terminal source
gap ending exactly at dataset_end_utc_exclusive.

Historical dataset auditing was corrected so:

internal gaps still require stored bars on both sides;

a leading gap beginning at dataset_start_utc does not require an
out-of-contract preceding bar;

a trailing gap ending at dataset_end_utc_exclusive does not require
an out-of-contract resumed bar;

missing-bar accounting includes leading and trailing gaps.

The corrected behavior was verified against the actual BICO frozen
manifest on OLD-BOX.
