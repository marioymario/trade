RESEARCH CONCLUSION

BTC is the sole PRIMARY_COMPARABLE member that survives the current campaign
rejection policy.

This is not deployment approval.

BTC's total profit and return-to-drawdown are modest.

Its strongest evidence is cross-period consistency:
all three frozen validation folds are positive.

The frozen scorer/strategy configuration is not cross-asset robust.

Seven of eight PRIMARY members fail at least one rejection rule.

Several rejected members remain profitable in aggregate, demonstrating why
aggregate PnL alone is insufficient for candidate advancement.

XLM is the strongest example:
large aggregate validation profit, but one losing validation fold.

FIXED-QUANTITY BASELINE INTERPRETATION CORRECTION

The earlier PRIMARY-8 baseline used 0.01 base-asset units per trade.

That run remains valid for sizing-independent behavioral evidence such as:

- trade chronology
- trade counts
- entry and exit events
- stop/exit frequencies
- signal behavior

It is not valid for economically comparable cross-asset dollar PnL.

Additionally, fold PnL signs are not sizing-independent.

Equal-USD sizing changes the relative monetary weight of trades across time,
even when entry and exit chronology is unchanged.

BTC demonstrates this directly:

Old fixed-quantity baseline:
approximately -0.70 USD total validation PnL
1/3 positive validation folds

New equal-USD-notional baseline:
+3.351793603110794 USD total validation PnL
3/3 positive validation folds

Therefore monetary fold metrics for cross-asset research must come from the
explicit comparable sizing contract.
