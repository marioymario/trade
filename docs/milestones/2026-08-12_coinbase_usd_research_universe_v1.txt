DATA INTEGRITY CONTRACT

Only actual Coinbase source observations are stored.

Synthetic candles are prohibited.

Forward-filled or interpolated OHLCV candles are prohibited.

Cross-exchange historical substitution is prohibited.

Missing source observations remain explicit gaps.

Historical replay must remain gap-aware.

Manifest-backed historical source identity is required.


ACQUISITION ARCHITECTURE

Strict complete-history acquisition remains preserved.

Universe V1 uses explicit gap-tolerant available-bar acquisition
for newly acquired datasets.

The acquisition layer validates and persists available observations.

The canonical historical manifest layer owns authoritative gap
discovery, grouping, and Coinbase 1-minute verification.

This separation was introduced after sparse ZEC historical data
demonstrated that recursively trying to force complete 5-minute
coverage caused pathological API work.
